    How your figures were produced

      Overtime is taxed with normal pay

      Extra overtime raises your gross income. The amount you keep depends on the tax and National Insurance bands your total income reaches.

      Paid leave can raise your effective hourly value

      When leave is paid, you receive normal pay for leave time without working those hours. Unpaid leave reduces your estimated gross pay.

      Real hourly pay includes your spending

      This app divides the money left after tax and entered yearly costs by the hours you actually work, including overtime.

      Use the result as an estimate

      Your tax code, pension, student loan, bonuses, benefits and payroll timing can make your real payslip different.

      Wage Wise stores no personal or financial data. Refreshing or closing the page clears the calculation.
